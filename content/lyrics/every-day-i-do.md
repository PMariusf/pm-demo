# Every Day, I Do

[Verse 1]
I’ve held your hand a thousand times,
But this one feels brand new.
A little tremble in your fingers,
And I’m trembling with you.

There’s so much I want to promise,
But I’ll start with something true:
I love the life we’ve made together,
And the life we’re walking into.

[Chorus]
For now, forever,
For all we haven’t been through,
I’ll stand beside you—
Every day, I do.

We join our lives, we make this vow,
Let love grow deeper than we knew.
With all I am, and all I’ll be,
Every day, I choose you—
Because I love you.

[Verse 2]
When the flowers leave the table,
When the dress is put away,
I will love you in the kitchen
At the end of an ordinary day.

We’ll make room for both our dreaming,
We’ll take turns being strong.
And when we struggle for the words,
I’ll stay and listen just as long.

[Chorus]
For now, forever,
For all we haven’t been through,
I’ll stand beside you—
Every day, I do.

We join our lives, we make this vow,
Let love grow deeper than we knew.
With all I am, and all I’ll be,
Every day, I choose you—
Because I love you.

[Bridge]
One day these hands will look much older,
And we’ll dance a little slow.
Put your head against my shoulder—
There’s still so much of us to know.

[Final Chorus]
For now, forever,
For all we haven’t been through,
I’ll stand beside you—
Every day, I do.

We joined our lives, we keep this vow
In all the little things we do.
With all I am, and all I’ll be,
Every day, I choose you—
Because I love you.

[Outro]
When this day becomes a memory,
And the years have carried through,
Ask me if I’d choose you over—
Every time, I do.

Because I love you.
