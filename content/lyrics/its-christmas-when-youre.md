# It's Christmas When You're

[Verse 1]

You come in with the winter cold,
Put your gloves beside the door.
There’s a box of tangled fairy lights
Spread out across the floor.

You hold the ladder while I reach,
Say the star is leaning right.
Then you laugh and tell me, “Leave it.”
And we finally switch on the lights.

[Chorus]

Let the snow fall down,
Let the candles glow.
Pull your chair a little closer;
We’ve got nowhere else to go.

All the things I meant to tell you
Seem so easy, warm and clear.
You are loved, and you are welcome—
It’s Christmas when you’re here.

[Verse 2]

There’s an old song in the kitchen,
And you’re singing out of tune.
Something’s burning in the oven,
But we’ll laugh about it soon.

We bring out the chipped red dishes,
Find a place for everyone.
And the stories round the table
Last until the food is gone.

[Chorus]

Let the snow fall down,
Let the candles glow.
Pull your chair a little closer;
We’ve got nowhere else to go.

All the things I meant to tell you
Seem so easy, warm and clear.
You are loved, and you are welcome—
It’s Christmas when you’re here.

[Bridge – Tender]

When we pack away the ribbons,
When the lights come down again,
Let us keep a little of this—
Making time and letting people in.

You can knock here any evening.
You don’t need a holiday.

[Final Chorus]

Let the snow fall down,
Let the candles glow.
Stay and have another coffee;
There’s no hurry to get home.

All the things I meant to tell you,
Let me say them loud and clear:
You are loved, and you are welcome—
It’s Christmas when you’re here.

[Outro – Soft]

Leave the star a little crooked.
Let it shine another year.
You are loved, and you are welcome.
It’s Christmas when you’re here.
