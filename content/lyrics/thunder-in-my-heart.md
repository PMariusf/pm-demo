# Thunder in My Heart

[Verse 1 – Low, Restrained]

I used to count the passing cars,
Their headlights moving through my room.
Another night of standing still,
With every thought leading back to you.

Tonight I reach beside the door,
Take down the coat I never wear.
My hands still shake around the keys,
But I can feel a change in the air.

[Pre-Chorus – Building]

One deep breath before I start.
One beat louder in the dark.

[Chorus – Powerful]

There’s thunder in my heart,
Lightning running through my veins.
I’ve been quiet for so long—
Now I feel it all again.

Let the sky break open,
Let the rain fall where we are.
I’ve still got love worth giving,
Still got thunder in my heart.

[Verse 2]

The road runs silver through the rain,
I pull aside to watch it fall.
I used to wish I could feel nothing,
Now I want to feel it all.

The love, the hurt, the nights we lost,
The hope I almost left behind.
I turn my face into the weather,
Let the cold remind me I’m alive.

[Pre-Chorus – Building]

I can miss you and still start.
There’s room for both inside this heart.

[Chorus – Powerful]

There’s thunder in my heart,
Lightning running through my veins.
I’ve been quiet for so long—
Now I feel it all again.

Let the sky break open,
Let the rain fall where we are.
I’ve still got love worth giving,
Still got thunder in my heart.

[Bridge – Vulnerable, Then Rising]

I wish you could have seen me
When I finally stepped outside.
Nothing in my life was settled,
But I didn’t turn and hide.

I let myself want something
That I hadn’t wanted in years—
A morning I could run toward,
A reason to be here.

[Final Chorus – Full Power]

There’s thunder in my heart,
Lightning running through my veins.
I’ve been quiet for so long—
You can hear me now again.

Let the sky break open,
I will meet it with open arms.
I’ve still got love worth giving,
Still got thunder in my heart.

[Outro – Soft]

The rain slows down.
The morning starts.
I’m still here—
With thunder in my heart.
