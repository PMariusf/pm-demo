# From Afar

[Verse 1]

Driving alone in my car,
Through the night and into day.
I turn the music down again,
Rehearse the words I never say.

I wonder how to walk up to you,
How to ask you how you’ve been.
Without my voice betraying
Everything I’ve kept within.

[Pre-Chorus]

In my head, the words come easy.
When you’re near, they disappear.

[Chorus]

My heart will forever belong to you,
Though you may never know.
I wish that you could understand
What I’ve carried for so long.

I don’t know how to cross this distance,
Or even where to start.
So much I wish that I could tell you,
While I love you from afar.

[Verse 2]

Walking alone through the city,
Hands buried in my coat.
I see you laughing in the distance;
There’s that aching in my throat.

I lift my hand, then let it fall.
The moment slips away.
You go on about your morning;
I think of you all day.

[Pre-Chorus]

Would you stop if I said hello?
Could I let the rest unfold?

[Chorus]

My heart will forever belong to you,
Though you may never know.
I wish that you could understand
What I’ve carried for so long.

I don’t know how to cross this distance,
Or even where to start.
So much I wish that I could tell you,
While I love you from afar.

[Bridge – Quiet, Then Rising]

You couldn’t hear what I never said,
Or read the thoughts inside my head.
I wish you knew, but how could you?
I’ve never let the words come through.

I don’t know what you’d say to me—
I only know this silence hurts.

[Final Chorus]

My heart will forever belong to you,
But the choice is yours to make.
I only want to tell you once,
Though my voice may bend and break.

I don’t need a perfect moment,
Just the courage to make a start.
To stand before you, say “I miss you,”
Instead of loving from afar.

[Outro – Soft]

Parked outside, the engine quiet.
One more thing I wish you knew—
Every road I took today,
I spent it thinking of you.
