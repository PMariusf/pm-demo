# Artist assets

- `public/images/Ayline/`: explicitly named Aylina images and the September 2 Ayline series. `Aylina.png` is the current homepage hero test.
- `public/images/Vexa/`: explicitly named Vexa images and the September 15 Vexa series.
- `public/images/PM/`: PM portrait.
- `public/images/Unsorted/`: images whose identity is unclear from their filenames. Preserved for review rather than assigned to the wrong artist.
- `public/videos/Ayline/`: rock-singer video.
- `public/videos/Unsorted/`: other videos awaiting identification. No assets were discarded.

Homepage image crops are set in `app/artist-images.css`. Adjust image paths and `background-position` there to test other portraits without stretching them.
